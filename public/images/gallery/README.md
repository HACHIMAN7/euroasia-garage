# Workshop Gallery Images

You can place your workshop photos directly in this folder!

### How to use:
1. Copy or upload your photos here (e.g. `workshop-1.jpg`, `engine.jpg`, `brake-bay.jpg`).
2. Update the photo paths in `resources/views/home.blade.php` under `$galleryPhotos` using:
   `asset('images/gallery/your-photo.jpg')`
3. Commit and push to GitHub — Render will automatically update your live site!
